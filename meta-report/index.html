Action list

  1. Fix conversion tracking first. The pixel's server-side signal has been dark since Jan 2025; CAPI Gateway is the single biggest opportunity (+28 points). Every ROAS figure here is measured through an under-reporting pixel.
  2. Refresh the "Construction Ad 12-7-23" creative. Top purchase driver in every window (62 purchases in July, 12 last week), but CTR is soft at ~0.33 to 0.35% and cost per purchase rose to $88.66 last week from $66.25 in July. Queue a new variant while it is still profitable at 3.7 to 5.0x ROAS.
  3. Scale the retargeting and lookalike winners. "Retargeting 4.7.2022", "Waterproof Paper II" and "Retargeting 4-5-23" sit well above the blended ROAS line across all ranges and several are budget-limited.
  4. Prune or consolidate the zero-return tail. The catch-all and CBO duplicates repeatedly spend with little return; Meta flags a fragmentation fix on Menu Boards worth ~18% lower cost per purchase.
  5. Trim desktop and dead placements. Desktop runs a ~0.05% CTR and $8 to $11 CPC in every window; right-hand column and marketplace add little. Shift toward mobile Feed and Reels.
  6. Push delivery into the cheap evening hours. 7 PM to 1 AM Pacific is consistently the best value; the 4 AM to 1 PM block (most of the budget) is the most expensive and least engaging.
  7. Turn on Advantage+ audience and 9:16 Reels video. Low-effort, compounding wins Meta flags across many ad sets, once tracking is fixed.
  8. Fix the two non-delivering ad sets. A combined detailed-targeting error is stalling delivery at no extra cost to resolve.
